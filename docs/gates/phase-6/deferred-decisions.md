# Phase 6 Deferred Decisions

These items are deliberately outside the implemented Phase 6 Engineering Gate.

1. **Governed real-user qualification.** Measure minimal-profile completion, comprehension,
   cognitive load and at least one real high-intent action with consented design partners.
2. **Production identity and privacy lifecycle.** Select real authentication, credential/session
   lifecycle, access/export/correction/deletion and account closure before public operation.
3. **Phase 5 real Gold qualification.** The independent 200-record real Gold evidence workflow is
   not replaced by three synthetic opportunities used in Phase 6 tests.
4. **Phase 7 feedback governance.** FeedbackEvent, review queue, adjudication, SLA and feedback-led
   release changes require a separate contract and Gate.
5. **Phase 8 notifications.** Reminder, Outbox, push, calendar, mini-program and multi-channel
   delivery remain unimplemented.
6. **Personalization models and retrieval.** No LLM, Model Gateway, vector similarity or learned
   ranker is selected; measured need and separate safety evaluation are required.
7. **Production hosting and operations.** Cloud persistence, backup/recovery, rate limits,
   monitoring, deployment, rollback and incident response remain undecided.
8. **Contract stability.** v0.5 remains `IMPLEMENTED`; only corresponding Release Qualification
   `QUALIFIED` evidence can support a later `STABLE` decision.
9. **Legal/compliance qualification.** Actual operator, features, region and scale require current
   professional review; this Gate package is not legal advice.

These deferrals do not convert into implementation blockers for unrelated engineering work, but
all applicable items block production qualification or expanded public use.
